A Fog of War Chess AI that can play based on Belief States

Fog of War Chess is a vairent of the classical board game chess. The piece moves and goal are identical to standard chess. The variation in Fog of War Chess is the introduction of a fog of war cloud, such that the player can only see the board where their own pieces can move to. Where chess is normally a fully observable statespace, fog of war creates a board of uncertainty, where players rely on probability to gain an advantage.
